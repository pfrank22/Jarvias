# Pricing Questions for Lincoln

Bring this to the pricing conversation. The answers go straight into `site/pricing.js`, and the website, calculator and print pieces update automatically. Numbers in *italics* are the current placeholders.

## 1. Product and cost
- [ ] Which product or supplier are we installing? (brand, white-label, or our own components)
- [ ] **Our cost per foot** for channel + LEDs, installed-ready. *($7.50/ft)*
- [ ] Cost of a **controller** and a **power supply**. How many feet does one power supply run?
- [ ] Channel colors available, and is color-matching included?
- [ ] Cold rating (−30°F / −40°F?) and waterproof rating (IP67 / IP68)?
- [ ] Minimum order, lead time, and whether stock ships from the US
- [ ] Does the supplier give us a dealer price list or volume tiers?

## 2. What we charge
- [ ] **Price per linear foot**, installed. *($29/ft)*
- [ ] **Minimum job.** *($2,495)*
- [ ] Extra for two-story / lift work? *(+12%)* Steep roofs or lots of peaks? *(+8%)*
- [ ] Price for an extra control zone *($295)*, accent/patio lighting *($24/ft)*, detached garage *($650)*
- [ ] Is the app/controller setup included in the per-foot price, or charged separately?

## 3. Warranty
- [ ] Product warranty from the supplier, and is it passed through to the customer? *(5 years)*
- [ ] Our labor warranty. *(2 years)*
- [ ] Offer an extended labor warranty? Price? *(10 years, $395)*
- [ ] Who pays for a service trip in years 1–2?

## 4. Labor
- [ ] Crew size and pay rate (with payroll costs). *(2 people, $32/hr loaded)*
- [ ] Feet a crew can install in one day. *(about 150–180 ft)*
- [ ] Do we own a lift, or rent one for two-story jobs?

## 5. Deals and payment
- [ ] Neighbor referral discount. *($250 off)*
- [ ] Off-season discount (Jan–Aug). *(7%)*
- [ ] Veteran / first responder discount. *(5%)*
- [ ] Largest total discount allowed. *(15%)*
- [ ] Deposit at signing. *(30–50%)*
- [ ] Financing partner? Rate, term and dealer fee. *(example: 9.99% APR, 60 months)*

## 6. Electrical
- [ ] When a new outlet is needed, who installs it and at what price? *($375 pass-through)*
- [ ] Has anyone confirmed with Minnesota DLI whether our installs need a Power Limited Technician license or a permit?
