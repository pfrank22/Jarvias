01 · Story

What the star stands for

Use this when you write an About page, a pitch or a radio spot.

For centuries, people found their way home by the North Star. Minnesota put it on the state seal and called itself L'Étoile du Nord. We put a little of that light on your house.

North Star installs permanent lighting that disappears into your trim by day and does whatever you want at night: warm white on a Tuesday, purple and gold on game day, red and green from Thanksgiving to New Year's. One install. No ladders, ever again.

Guiding

We design the layout with you, show you a night mockup, and teach you the app before we leave.

Built for here

Rated for −30°F nights and ice dams. Local crew, local phone number, January service calls.

Constant

A permanent fixture of the house and of the neighborhood. The light your street looks for.

Clear space

Keep space equal to the star's width on all sides.

Minimum size

Mark 24 px on screen, 0.5 in in print. Below that, use the mark without the wordmark.

Don't

Stretch, outline, recolor the star, or put the full-color mark on busy photos.

Don't

Shorten to "North Star Lighting" in public. Always include "Holiday."
