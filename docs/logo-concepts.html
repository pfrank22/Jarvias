Six new logo directions

Same name, six different ideas. Each one is shown large on night blue, on white, and at app-icon and browser-tab size, because most logos fail when they're small. Pick one or two favorites, or mix parts ("the badge from 1 with the font from 4").

How to choose

Picture it in three places: on the side of the van at 40 mph, as a yard sign from across the street, and as a tiny icon in Google search results. The one that still reads in all three wins. My picks are 2 (Star Window) for warmth and story, and 4 (Big Dipper) as the most distinctive and ownable.
