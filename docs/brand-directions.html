Three ways to brand a permanent-lighting company for Minnesota

Each direction is a complete starting point: name, mark, palette, type, voice and how it looks on a yard sign and a van. Pick one to build out, or mix the name of one with the look of another.

Product
Permanent RGB roofline lighting, app-controlled, for every holiday and every night
Market
Homeowners within about 70 miles of Minneapolis–St. Paul
Competitors
National franchises and dealers: Trimlight, JellyFish, Gemstone, Outdoor Lighting Perspectives, Blingle
Our edge
Local and independent, built for −30°F winters, craftsmanship over franchise scripts

Why not North Star

Northstar Trimlight in Rochester sells exactly this product (permanent, programmable roofline lighting) and has since 2019. Rochester sits at the edge of a 70-mile radius from Minneapolis, and Trimlight also has dealers in Minneapolis, St. Paul and Mankato. A "North Star" permanent-lighting brand would be confused with them on Google and on yard signs. Choosing a new name now, before anything is printed, costs nothing.

Direction 1 · Premium tech

Borealis

Northern lights for your home. Every color, every season, from the app on your phone.

Why it works

  • Says color-changing light without saying "Christmas." Fits Halloween, the 4th and game day just as well.
  • Minnesota sees the aurora, so the name feels local without saying "MN."
  • Sounds premium and modern, which supports $25–35 per foot pricing.
  • Easy to say and spell. Grows into commercial, HOA and landscape lighting.
  • Risk: "Borealis" is a common word in other industries, so a trademark check matters. "Borealis Lighting Co." is the safer full name.

Direction 2 · Architectural luxury

Eaveline

Invisible by day. Unforgettable by night. Lighting that looks like it came with the house.

Why it works

  • Names where the product lives: the eave line. The name explains itself once someone sees an install.
  • A coined word, so it's the most ownable of the three. The domain and trademark are the likeliest to be free.
  • Positions you above the franchises: a design company, not a "Christmas light guy."
  • Suits high-end homes in Edina, Wayzata, Minnetonka and North Oaks, and partnerships with builders and architects.
  • Risk: less festive. Holiday excitement has to come from photos and campaigns, not the name.

Direction 3 · Minnesota proud

Ten Thousand Lights

Light up the Land of 10,000 Lakes. A local crew, built for real Minnesota winters.

Why it works

  • A clear nod to "Land of 10,000 Lakes." Minnesotans get it instantly and it makes people smile.
  • Stands out against national franchises: we're from here.
  • The badge looks great on hats, stickers and van doors, so people wear the brand.
  • Room for local campaigns: "10,000 homes lit," Winter Carnival, State Fair.
  • Risk: long name. Use "10K Lights" as a short form on social media and in the domain.

Recommendation: Borealis

It is the only name that sells the product's main benefit, every color for every occasion, while sounding local and premium. Against "Trimlight" and "JellyFish," a Minnesota-born name with a strong visual (the aurora ribbon) stands out. If you want the safest trademark position, Eaveline is the runner-up. If you want the warmest local word of mouth, choose Ten Thousand Lights.

Before you commit

  1. Search the Minnesota Secretary of State registry for the name.
  2. Search the USPTO trademark database, classes 11 (lighting) and 37 (installation).
  3. Check the .com and the Instagram and Facebook handles.
  4. Show the three logos to five customers in your target area. Ask which company they'd call.
