# Launch Kit

Everything you need to open for business: name ideas, brand basics, marketing copy, a customer agreement, and crew checklists.

---

## 1. Business name: North Star Holiday Lighting

- **Legal name:** North Star Lighting Co. (or the closest available variant)
- **Brand used on signs, site, and ads:** North Star Holiday Lighting
- **Later divisions:** North Star Permanent Lighting, North Star Landscape Lighting

**Why:** Minnesota is the North Star State, so the name feels local. Because the legal name isn't tied to Christmas, you can add permanent lighting and summer services later without renaming.

**Known nearby name conflicts (checked Nov 2026):**
- *Northstar Trimlight* (Rochester, MN) sells permanent roofline lighting. **Avoid marketing in the Rochester area as "North Star"**, or add your town to the name there.
- *Northstar Home Technologies* (Oakdale, MN) does audio/video. Low risk.
- An existing *NORTHSTAR LIGHTING* trademark covers lighting fixtures. Don't use plain "North Star Lighting" as your brand. Keep "Holiday" (or your town) in it.

**Before you file, check:**
- [ ] Minnesota Secretary of State business search (mblsportal.sos.mn.gov): "North Star Lighting," "North Star Holiday"
- [ ] Register an assumed name (DBA) for "North Star Holiday Lighting" if it differs from the LLC name (about $50 in MN)
- [ ] A domain: try northstarholidaylighting.com, northstarholiday.com, or northstarlights[town].com
- [ ] Facebook and Instagram handles (e.g., @northstarholidaylights)
- [ ] Google Maps search for "North Star" within 30 miles

**Alternates if the registry check fails:** North Star Lamplighters · [Town] North Star Lights · Evening Star Lighting

## 2. Brand basics

- **Colors:** midnight navy `#0A1628`, warm gold `#F5C25B`, plus accents in holiday red `#D8434E` and pine `#2F8F63`
- **Fonts:** Fraunces (headlines), Inter (body). Both are free on Google Fonts
- **Voice:** warm, confident, low-hassle. Sell the outcome ("your house, glowing") and the freedom ("without the ladder"), not the bulbs
- **Logo:** a gold four-point star above a roofline outlined in bulbs (`site/logo.svg`)
- **Tagline options:**
  - "Your house, glowing. Without the ladder."
  - "We hang it. We fix it. We store it."
  - "The brightest house on the block, done for you."
- **Truck and shirts:** logo, tagline, phone number, website. Large and readable from 50 feet

**Season:** 20__–20__  **Agreement term:** ☐ 1 season ☐ 3 seasons

**2. Price and payment.** Total seasonal price: $________ (plus applicable tax). A **50% non-refundable deposit** reserves the install date. The balance is due on the install day. Accounts more than 15 days late may be charged 1.5% per month, and the Display may be removed.

**4. Schedule.** Install is planned for the window of __________. Takedown begins after January 1 and is normally completed by January 15. Dates may shift because of weather or safety conditions.

**5. Maintenance.** The Company will repair Display malfunctions at no charge, normally within 48 hours of notice, from installation until December 31. Damage caused by the Customer, pets, landscapers, severe weather, or third parties may be billed at $____ per visit.

**6. Power.** The Customer provides working outdoor GFCI outlets. The Company does not perform electrical wiring. If new outlets are needed, the Customer will hire a licensed electrician at their own cost.

**7. Property.** The Company uses non-penetrating clips and will take reasonable care of the property. **8. Access.** The Customer grants the Company access to the property's exterior and roof for installation, service, and removal. The Customer will tell the Company about pets, gate codes, sprinklers, and buried lines.

**9. Multi-season terms (if selected).** The Customer agrees to renew each season at the stated price, with up to __% annual adjustment. Either party may cancel future seasons with written notice before September 1.

**10. Cancellation.** If the Customer cancels before the install, the deposit covers custom-cut materials and scheduling. If the Customer cancels after the install, the full price is due.

**11. HOA.** The Customer is responsible for any HOA approvals.

## 6. Crew checklists

### Quote / measure visit
- [ ] Measure the roofline in linear feet with a measuring wheel or satellite tool (Google Earth, Hover, or EagleView)
- [ ] Note roof pitch, number of stories, and gutter type (K-style or half-round)
- [ ] Count peaks, windows, trees (estimate their height), and bushes
- [ ] Find outdoor outlets and test them for GFCI and power. Note the circuits
- [ ] Take photos of the house front and of any existing damage
- [ ] Ask about color preference, HOA rules, pets, gate codes, and sprinklers
- [ ] Build the quote in the calculator and send it the same day

### Install day: before leaving the shop
- [ ] Customer bin pulled (returning customers) or runs pre-cut (new customers)
- [ ] Extra bulbs (10%), clips (15%), extension cords, timers, zip ties
- [ ] Ladders, stabilizers, harness and anchor, tool belt, tape, multimeter / GFCI tester
- [ ] Weather check: no frost, ice, rain, or wind above 20 mph on the roof

### Install day: on site
- [ ] Check in with the customer and confirm the design
- [ ] Take "before" photos, including any existing damage
- [ ] Set up ladders and place stabilizers
- [ ] Run lights from the power source outward. Keep connections elevated and wrapped
- [ ] Check bulb spacing is even and lines are straight; sight down the roofline
- [ ] Power test everything. Set the timer (default dusk to 11 PM)
- [ ] Clean up: no clips, bulbs, or packaging left in the yard
- [ ] Take "after" photos at dusk for marketing
- [ ] Place the yard sign (with permission)
- [ ] Collect the balance. Send the review request tonight
- [ ] Door hangers on 10 neighbors' doors

### Takedown
- [ ] Remove the lights section by section and label each run (e.g., "SMITH – front gutter left")
- [ ] Coil without kinks. Discard and log any bad sockets or bulbs
- [ ] Pack everything into the customer's labeled bin with their design sheet on top
- [ ] Walk the yard and gutters for stray clips
- [ ] Offer a renewal agreement at early-bird pricing
