## 1. Name and legal setup (Minnesota)

| Name | Use |
|---|---|
| **North Star Lighting LLC** | Legal entity: contracts, bank, insurance, taxes |
| **North Star Holiday Lighting** | Assumed name (DBA): everything customers see |
| **northstarholidaylighting.com** | Website, email, QR codes |
| North Star Landscape Lighting / North Star Commercial | Future assumed names, Year 2+ |

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Minnesota Secretary of State business search (mblsportal.sos.mn.gov) for "North Star Lighting"
- [ ] File the LLC Articles of Organization (about $155 online)
- [ ] File the assumed name "North Star Holiday Lighting" (about $50) and **publish it in a qualified legal newspaper for 2 consecutive issues**
- [ ] Get an EIN (free, irs.gov). Open a business bank account that accepts both names
- [ ] Register northstarholidaylighting.com, plus northstarholidaylights.com to redirect
- [ ] Claim @northstarholidaylights on Instagram and Facebook
- [ ] **Call Minnesota DLI Electrical Licensing** about low-voltage permanent lighting (see the business plan, section 7)
- [ ] Get general liability insurance, and workers' comp before hiring
- [ ] Optional: a USPTO trademark application for "North Star Holiday Lighting" plus the logo (classes 37 and 11). Talk to a trademark attorney first; there's an existing NORTHSTAR LIGHTING mark for fixtures

**Rule:** never advertise as just "North Star Lighting." Always include "Holiday." That keeps us clear of Northstar Trimlight (Rochester) and the existing fixture trademark.

## 2. Brand basics

- **Mark:** the North Star above a roof peak, with a line of aurora-colored lights under the eave
- **Colors:** Northern Night `#0A1424` · Polaris Gold `#FFC94A` · Snow `#F2F5F7` · Ice `#9AAABB` · Aurora `#3CE6A2 → #2BB8E0 → #8B6BFF` (use the aurora only for the lights)
- **Type:** Archivo (free on Google Fonts). Expanded Black for headlines, Regular for body
- **Taglines:** "Light the way home." (primary) · "Every holiday. No ladder." · "Hidden by day. Brilliant by night." · "Star of the North."
- **Story:** Minnesota's motto is *L'Étoile du Nord*, Star of the North. People have always found their way home by the North Star. We put a little of that light on your house.
- **Voice:** a good neighbor who happens to be an expert. Plain, warm, a little dry about winter.

## 5. Crew checklists

### Design visit (about 30 minutes)
- [ ] Measure the roofline runs the customer wants: front, garage, sides (measuring wheel plus Google Earth)
- [ ] Note stories, pitch, fascia vs. soffit mount, and fascia condition (rot or loose boards = repair first)
- [ ] Find outlets and test GFCI. Plan the power supply location and wire route
- [ ] Match the trim color with the channel color samples
- [ ] Check Wi-Fi strength where the controller will go
- [ ] Show the demo board and the night mockup. Set the favorite scenes
- [ ] Price it in `index.html?owner`. Send the quote the same day. Offer financing

### Install day
- [ ] Load-out: channel (+10%), pixel runs, power supplies, controller, stainless screws, sealant, end caps, spare sections
- [ ] Ladders, stabilizers, harness and anchors, lift (if two-story), miter saw, drills, multimeter
- [ ] Walk the house with the customer. Take "before" photos, including the fascia condition
- [ ] Cut and dry-fit the channel. Keep spacing consistent and miter the peaks cleanly
- [ ] Mount the channel, seat the pixels, seal the entries, hide wire in the soffit or behind downspouts
- [ ] Mount the power supply. Connect to GFCI. Test every zone and every pixel
- [ ] Set up the app on the customer's phone: schedule (sunset to 11 PM), 5 favorite scenes, plus voice assistant if requested
- [ ] Walkthrough: switch scenes, change the schedule, who to text for service
- [ ] Clean up. Take "after" photos at dusk. Put out the yard sign and 20 door hangers
- [ ] Collect the balance or confirm financing. Send the review request tonight

### Service call
- [ ] Ask for photos or video first. Many issues are Wi-Fi or the app (fix remotely)
- [ ] Carry a spare pixel section, power supply and controller
- [ ] Log the cause (product / install / external) for warranty tracking
