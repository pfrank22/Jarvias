Questions, answered

When do you install and take down?

Installs run from late October through mid-December. Takedown starts the first week of January. You choose your dates when you book.

Do I have to buy the lights?

No. Most customers lease: we own the lights, maintain them, and store them each year. You can also buy them for about 35% more and they're yours after takedown.

Will you damage my roof or gutters?

Never. We use plastic clips that grip shingles and gutters. No nails, staples, or screws. We're fully insured.

What if a bulb goes out?

Call or text us. Service calls are free all season, and we usually fix it within 48 hours.

Can I use my own lights?

Yes. We can hang customer-owned lights at our labor rate. We can't guarantee their performance, so we recommend ours.

Do you need power or outlets?

We use your existing outdoor outlets on GFCI-protected circuits. If you need a new outlet, we'll arrange a licensed electrician.

Do you work with HOAs?

Yes. We'll give you a design mockup to submit for approval, and we can do matching displays for a whole street.
