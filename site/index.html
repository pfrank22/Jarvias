Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Can you see the lights during the day?

Barely. The channel is about an inch wide and color-matched to your trim, so from the street it reads as part of the house.

Do they really work at −30°F?

Yes. The LEDs and connectors are sealed and rated for extreme cold. Snow on top doesn't matter because the lights sit underneath, inside the channel.

How long do they last?

The LEDs are rated for about 50,000 hours, more than 15 years at 8 hours a night. You're covered by a warranty, with a 10-year labor option.

Will it damage my roof, soffit or gutters?

No. We fasten the channel to the fascia or soffit with stainless screws, never through your shingles, and seal every penetration.

Do I need a new outlet?

Usually not. The system plugs into an existing outdoor or garage outlet through a low-voltage power supply. If you need a new outlet, our licensed electrician partner installs it.

How do I control them?

With a free phone app. Set schedules (on at sunset, off at 11), pick scenes, or make your own. Alexa and Google Home work too.

Can you install in winter?

Yes, on dry days. We avoid icy or snow-covered roofs. Off-season installs (January–August) get 7% off and faster scheduling.

What if we sell the house?

Permanent lighting stays with the home and is a selling feature. We'll transfer the warranty to the new owners.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, with approved credit. Most customers choose a monthly payment that's less than a streaming bundle.
